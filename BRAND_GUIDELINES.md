# Outpouring Missions International — Brand Guidelines

**Version 1.0 · Established 2008**
**Parent Brand:** Outpouring Missions International (OMI)
**Sub-Brands:** YES (Youth Empowerment School) · YES Rise Up

> Transforming Lives. Igniting Futures.

---

## 1. Brand Essence

### 1.1 Mission Statement
Outpouring Missions International exists to **empower, equip, and expand** the reach of faith-driven leaders through education, mentorship, and global outreach — transforming lives and igniting futures across nations.

### 1.2 Brand Pillars
| Pillar | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Faith** | Rooted in conviction; every initiative is purpose-led. |
| **Education** | Learning is the lever — from classrooms to conferences. |
| **Leadership** | Raising servant-leaders who lead from the front. |
| **Impact** | Measured results in lives, communities, and nations. |

### 1.3 Brand Promises
- **Empower** — release potential in people.
- **Equip** — arm them with tools, truth, and training.
- **Expand** — extend reach across borders and generations.

### 1.4 Voice & Tone
- **Confident, not boastful.** We speak with quiet authority.
- **Warm, not casual.** Hospitality in every sentence.
- **Purposeful, not preachy.** Lead with outcomes, not rhetoric.
- **Global, not generic.** Local texture, international posture.

**Do say:** *"We equip leaders to rise."*
**Don't say:** *"We're the best missions org out there."*

## 2. Logo System

### 2.1 Core Mark
The OMI monogram — stylized **"O + M"** rendered as an upward-thrusting chevron — symbolizes **ascent, outpouring, and forward mission**. Indigo and gold ribbons converge at the peak to signal unity of faith and action.

### 2.2 Lockups
| Lockup | Use |
|---|---|
| **Core Mark** | App icons, favicons, social avatars, small-scale uses. |
| **Full Brand Lockup** | Primary use on web, print, letterhead, signage. |
| **Seal (Est. 2008)** | Credentials, certificates, formal documents, legacy pieces. |
| **Sub-brand Lockups** | YES, YES Rise Up — always paired with their own wordmark. |

### 2.3 Clear Space
Minimum clear space around any lockup equals the **height of the "O" in Outpouring**. Never place text, imagery, or edges closer.

### 2.4 Minimum Sizes
- Core Mark: **24 px** digital / **0.25 in** print
- Full Lockup: **120 px** digital / **1.25 in** print
- Seal: **64 px** digital / **0.75 in** print

### 2.5 Don'ts
- Don't rotate, skew, or re-color the mark outside the approved palette.
- Don't place the mark on low-contrast backgrounds without the white or charcoal variant.
- Don't add drop shadows, outer glows, or bevels.
- Don't re-typeset the wordmark — always use the approved artwork.

## 3. Color System

The OMI palette is **deep, confident, and celestial** — built around a midnight indigo foundation, warmed by heritage gold, and activated by mission teal.

### 3.1 Primary Palette

| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK | Pantone (nearest) | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Deep Indigo** | `#0B1F3D` | 11, 31, 61 | 100, 89, 45, 55 | PMS 2767 C | Primary backgrounds, headlines, authority surfaces |
| **Royal Indigo** | `#152C5B` | 21, 44, 91 | 98, 85, 30, 25 | PMS 2756 C | Secondary surfaces, gradients, hover states |
| **Heritage Gold** | `#D4A24C` | 212, 162, 76 | 15, 35, 80, 5 | PMS 7563 C | Accents, seals, CTAs, premium moments |
| **Bright Gold** | `#F2C54A` | 242, 197, 74 | 5, 22, 80, 0 | PMS 1235 C | Highlights, hover glow, gradient tips |
| **Mission Teal** | `#17A4C2` | 23, 164, 194 | 78, 20, 20, 0 | PMS 7710 C | Sub-brand (YES), links, active states |
| **Electric Teal** | `#00C2E0` | 0, 194, 224 | 75, 5, 10, 0 | PMS 3125 C | YES Rise Up, youth-facing energy |

### 3.2 Neutrals

| Name | HEX | RGB | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pure White** | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Reversed type, clean surfaces |
| **Off White** | `#F4F6FA` | 244, 246, 250 | Light backgrounds, cards |
| **Mist** | `#D9DEE8` | 217, 222, 232 | Dividers, subtle borders |
| **Graphite** | `#2A2D34` | 42, 45, 52 | Secondary dark surfaces |
| **Charcoal** | `#141518` | 20, 21, 24 | Deep dark surfaces, footer |

### 3.3 Gradients
- **Celestial** — `#0B1F3D → #152C5B → #17A4C2` — hero backgrounds, banners.
- **Sunrise** — `#D4A24C → #F2C54A` — accent sweeps, button glow.
- **Horizon** — `#0B1F3D → #17A4C2 → #F2C54A` — full-brand storytelling moments (use sparingly).

### 3.4 Color Ratios
A typical composition:
- **60%** Indigo family (Deep + Royal)
- **25%** Neutrals (White, Off White, Charcoal)
- **10%** Gold family
- **5%** Teal family

### 3.5 Accessibility
All text must meet **WCAG 2.2 AA contrast**.
- Gold on Deep Indigo: ✅ passes
- White on Deep Indigo: ✅ passes
- Teal on White: ✅ passes (large text only below 4.5:1 for body — use Royal Indigo for body)
- Gold on White: ⚠️ only for large display type, never body.

## 4. Typography

> **Note on font sourcing.** The reference brand board was an AI-rendered composite, so the letterforms on it are approximations rather than a licensed typeface. The fonts below are the closest **real, production-ready** matches — all free/open via Google Fonts, with premium upgrade paths called out where relevant.

### 4.1 Primary Typefaces
| Role | Typeface | License | Match to reference | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Display / Wordmark** | **Montserrat** (900 Black) | Google Fonts · OFL | Round geometric O, flat terminals, near-black weight | Primary wordmark + all headlines |
| **Subtitle / Tracked Caps** | **Montserrat** (400 Regular, `+0.2em` tracking) | Google Fonts · OFL | Matches "MISSIONS INTERNATIONAL" treatment | Eyebrows, dividers |
| **Body / UI** | **Inter** (400 / 500 / 600) | Google Fonts · OFL | Neutral pairing with Montserrat | Long-form, UI |
| **Athletic Sub-brand (YES)** | **Archivo Black** (italicized via `font-style: italic`) · or **Saira Condensed** 900 Italic | Google Fonts · OFL | Chunky block italic seen on "YES" | Free alternative to premium **Druk Wide Italic** (Commercial Type) |
| **Script / Tagline** | **Kaushan Script** | Google Fonts · OFL | Brushed, painterly — matches "Igniting Futures." | Use only for the tagline flourish |
| **Monospace** | **JetBrains Mono** | Google Fonts · OFL | Technical labels, code, data |

**Premium upgrade path (optional, licensed):**
- Display wordmark → **Gotham Ultra** or **Proxima Nova Black** (Hoefler&Co / Mark Simonson)
- Athletic YES → **Druk Wide Italic** (Commercial Type) or **Industry Inc** (Fort Foundry)
- Script → **Playlist Script** or **Thirsty Rough**

### 4.2 Google Fonts Embed
Paste in `<head>`:
```html
<link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.googleapis.com">
<link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.gstatic.com" crossorigin>
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat:wght@400;500;600;700;800;900&family=Inter:wght@400;500;600;700&family=Archivo+Black&family=Saira+Condensed:wght@900&family=Kaushan+Script&family=JetBrains+Mono:wght@400;500&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```

### 4.3 Type Scale (desktop · 16 px base)
| Token | Size / Line | Weight | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `display-xl` | 72 / 80 | 900 | Hero / wordmark-adjacent |
| `display-lg` | 56 / 64 | 800 | Section hero |
| `h1` | 44 / 52 | 800 | Page title |
| `h2` | 32 / 40 | 700 | Section title |
| `h3` | 24 / 32 | 700 | Subsection |
| `h4` | 20 / 28 | 600 | Card title |
| `body-lg` | 18 / 28 | 400 | Lead paragraph |
| `body` | 16 / 26 | 400 | Default body |
| `body-sm` | 14 / 22 | 400 | Captions, meta |
| `label` | 12 / 16 | 600 / tracked +0.08em | Eyebrow, button label |

### 4.4 Typographic Rules
- **Wordmark + headlines** — Montserrat 800–900, tight tracking (`-0.01em`), sentence case by default.
- **Eyebrows / Kickers** — Montserrat 600, uppercase, `+0.08em` tracking (`+0.2em` for the "MISSIONS INTERNATIONAL" style subtitle).
- **YES wordmark** — Archivo Black (or Saira Condensed 900), italicized, paired with a chevron accent on the "Y".
- **Tagline "Igniting Futures"** — Kaushan Script in Heritage Gold, only as flourish, never body.
- **Never** use all-caps for long runs of text (accessibility + tone).
- **Numerals** — lining figures for data tables, oldstyle for editorial.

## 7. Imagery & Photography

### 7.1 Subject
- **People first** — faces, hands, action. Candid > posed.
- **Global texture** — architecture, landscapes, local craft.
- **Education in motion** — classrooms, mentorship, graduations.

### 7.2 Treatment
- **Warm shadows, cool highlights** — cinematic but natural.
- **Slight teal-orange split-tone** acceptable for hero imagery.
- **Never** over-saturate; never apply heavy filters.
- **Avoid** stock imagery with visible clichés (generic handshakes, white-background studio).

### 7.3 Overlay Treatment
When placing type on imagery, use a **Deep Indigo 60% gradient from bottom** or a **celestial radial vignette** to protect legibility.

---

## 8. Iconography & Illustration

- **Line weight:** 1.75 px at 24 px grid.
- **Corners:** 2 px radius, rounded caps/joins.
- **Style:** geometric, consistent with Sora's geometry.
- **Illustration:** minimal, flat, two-tone (Indigo + Gold) with teal accents.
- **Never** mix icon styles within a single view.

---

## 9. Motion

- **Easing:** `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` (outExpo-like) for entrances.
- **Duration:** 200 ms UI, 400–600 ms storytelling, 800 ms+ hero reveal.
- **Signature move:** the *Outpouring sweep* — a gold-to-teal gradient wipe rising from bottom-left to top-right, used once per page maximum.
- **Never** bounce, flash, or spin for decoration alone.
